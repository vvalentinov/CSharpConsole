# 📅 Age Calculator App

Welcome to the **Age Calculator**! 🎉 This simple yet powerful C# console app helps you calculate your age in multiple units based on your birthdate. Whether you're curious about how many years, months, days, hours, or minutes you've lived, this app has you covered! 🌟

### ✨ Features:

- **Age Calculation**: Get your age in:  
    - Years 👶
    - Months 🗓️
    - Days 🏃
    - Hours ⏰
    - Minutes ⏳
- **User Input Validation**: Ensures your birthdate is entered correctly and is not in the future! 🛑
- **Friendly Prompts**: Get clear, helpful instructions and error messages to guide you along the way. 💬
- **Culture-Aware Parsing**: Handles date formats with CultureInfo.InvariantCulture for consistency across locales. 🌍

### 🚀 How It Works:

1. Enter your birthdate in the format yyyy-MM-dd 📅.
2. The program will validate the date and check if it’s in the future. ⏳
3. It calculates your age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes. 🧑‍💻
4. You’ll see your age displayed in all these units! 🎉
